## Spool Dessicant Container Print

To keep filament dry in airtight containers, I decided to rely on spool containers. I figured they would be easier and more practical than some of the container-specific designs that sit on the bottom and hold the filament roles. I printed these with PETG Black thinking they would withstand heat better if I wanted to keep dessicant in them during a drying cycle.

First I printed a set of the high flow, fine mesh spools, but then I switched to the high flow, standard mesh after seeing the actual mesh size. I changed my mind after starting the second print, so I tried to "stop print" option on the printer and learned it really does stop immediately and then you kind of need to reposition the print head and plate.

## Multi-color Paris Cityscape Print

I tried out another cityscape print of Paris - also part of my plans to make city prints for some of the destination cities I have traveled to. This was my first single plate, multi-color print, and I used PLA Gray, White, Green, and Black.

* The top of the Eiffel Tower ended up being a bit squiggly.
* I was worried about the bridges being extra fragile, but was surprised at the actual strength.
