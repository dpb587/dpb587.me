After moving around some furniture and making space for the new setup, it was finally time to unbox and unwrap. It was mostly straightforward, aside from a couple quirks:

* When attaching the touchscreen, one of the edge lips of the plastic cable holder snapped off even though I was being carefully. It might be an inconvenience if/when I need to remove the cable in the future, but it didn't seem to affect anything else.
* My first sample print attempt from the touchscreen failed with an "unrecognized plate" error. A Reddit suggestion proved correct to power cycle, update the firmware, and try again. Interestingly, I had gone to check the firmware for updates beforehand, but didn't see an option; after rebooting it became available. After the update, it wanted another calibration for vibration/noise and then it was ready.

## First Print

## Filament Spool Tires

I saw recommendations that cardboard filament spools (like the ones packaged with Inland) should not be used directly, at least not for long. Over time, the cardboard wears down from the rollers and can cause more dust instead of the AMS. For a more practical test, I printed a pair of these tires. Despite some reviews saying they didn't fit the AMS or scratched it, they worked fine.
