## 3D Modeling

I started looking into 3D modeling tools to understand how difficult it will be to learn my own. Downloaded and briefly tried Blender.

* https://www.reddit.com/r/3Dprinting/wiki/makingmodels/ - useful wiki page with some starter recommendations

* https://openscad.org/ - code-/function-based 3D modeling sounds interesting; last release in 2021?
