Every year, Albuquerque hosts hundreds of hot air balloons from around the world. Starting the first weekend of October through the following weekend, the [Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta](https://www.balloonfiesta.com/) is one of my favorite events. I attended a couple years before moving to Albuquerque, and, now that I live here, I regularly bike in to attend a few of the events every year.
